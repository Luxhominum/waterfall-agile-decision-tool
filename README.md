# ? Waterfall vs Agile vs Hybrid — Interactive Decision & Comparison Tool

An interactive, research-grounded web application designed to help project managers, engineers, and decision-makers systematically select the right project management methodology (Waterfall, Agile, or Hybrid Water-Scrum-Fall) based on empirical criteria.

> **Academic Foundation**: Based on the research paper by Theo Thesing, Carsten Feldmann, and Martin Burchardt: *" Agile versus Waterfall Project Management: Decision Model for Selecting the Appropriate Approach to a Project\*, **Procedia Computer Science 181 (2021) 746–756**.

---

## ?? Key Features

1. **?? Full Reactive Bilingual Support (Indonesian ID & English EN)**: Instant 1-click language switching across all metrics, forms, criteria, and recommendations without page reload.
2. **?? Comprehensive 5-Dimension Core Comparison**:
 - Scope & Quality Flexibility
 - Organizational & Governance Readiness
 - Project Team Autonomy & Dynamics
 - Time to Market & Milestones
 - Budget & Cost Predictability
 - *Plus*: Dedicated **Hybrid Model (Water-Scrum-Fall)** breakdown.
3. **?? Visual Process Flow Architectures**:
 - Interactive Stage-Gate Waterfall Pipeline (Phased milestones, high ceremony).
 - Cyclic Sprint Cadence Engine (Backlog refinement, 2-week iterations, continuous feedback).
4. **?? Empirical Practitioner Survey Data**:
 - Real-world advantages and constraints derived from industry surveys in Thesing et al. (2021).
5. **?? 2-Step Decision Engine**:
 - **Step 1 (Knockout Criteria)**: Evaluates 5 hard exclusion filters (Safety criticality, Monolithic non-decomposable scope, Fixed-firm contracts, Increment absorption limits, Single-release dependencies).
 - **Step 2 (15 Weighted Multi-Criteria Assessment)**: Interactive slider & selection inputs with dynamic live Chart.js Radar visualization.
 - **Quick Presets**: 1-click industry scenario templates (*E-Commerce Startup, Core Banking System, Smart IoT Hardware-Software*).
