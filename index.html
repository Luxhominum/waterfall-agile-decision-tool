Five-Dimensional Evaluation Matrix

Scope, Quality & Risk Management

Weight in Model: ~36% (Highest Priority)
Dim 01
Waterfall Fixed Scope

Requirements frozen upfront via formal BRD/SRS. Formal Change Request (CR) governance with cost/schedule impact approvals. Quality verified late in dedicated test phase.

Agile Emergent Scope

Dynamic backlog ranked by ROI. Embraces late requirement evolution as competitive advantage. Built-in quality through continuous integration, TDD, and sprint DoD.

Hybrid Nuance (Water-Scrum-Fall):

Upfront system architecture and compliance boundaries defined rigidly (Stage-Gate), while detailed feature components and UI modules iterate in 2-week sprint cadences.

Organizational Culture & Governance

Weight in Model: ~27%
Dim 02
Waterfall Fit: Hierarchical Governance

Thrives in environments demanding strict audit trails, heavy documentation, and formal steering committee approvals before capital release.

Agile Fit: High-Trust Empowerment

Demands decentralized authority, psychological safety for continuous learning, and active daily customer/sponsor co-ownership.

Project Team Dynamics

Weight in Model: ~21%
Dim 03
Specialized Functional Silos

Functional handovers (Analysts -> Architects -> Devs -> QA). Can effectively coordinate large distributed vendors (100+ members).

Cross-Functional Self-Organization

Dedicated, multi-disciplinary pods (5-9 members) with direct end-to-end delivery capabilities. High tacit knowledge over written handoffs.

Time to Market & Budget Predictability

Weight in Model: ~8% Time + ~8% Cost
Dim 04-05
Fixed-Price / Single Release

Targeted single delivery event. Budget estimated upfront for total project scope. High financial risk if late-stage defects require structural redesign.

ROI Realization: At project finale (100%)
Incremental Value & Burn Rate

Early working software in 2-4 weeks. Fixed timebox sprints with flexible scope allows stopping funding when sufficient ROI is achieved.

ROI Realization: Progressive every sprint (Continuous)
